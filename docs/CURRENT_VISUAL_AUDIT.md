# Current Visual Audit

Audit date: 2026-08-04

Local preview: `http://localhost:3010`

Baseline screenshots saved in `docs/cinematic-audit/`.

## Summary

The current site is functional and already contains key routes, forms, calculators, disclosures, WhatsApp handoff and founder content. The visual system, however, still reads as a dense component library rather than a premium consultancy website. The homepage especially has too many visible sections, too many repeated card treatments and no cinematic visual hook.

## Baseline Metrics

- Homepage: 13 sections, 44 paragraphs, 18 card-like blocks, 3 images, no video.
- Services page: 17 service cards, useful but visually long on mobile.
- Business loan page: strong SEO content, but service detail still feels templated.
- EMI page: logic works, but the visual surface needs a more premium workspace.
- Apply/contact pages: validated form is good, but surrounding page composition is plain.

## Current Problems

- Hero is a styled financial dashboard, not a cinematic brand moment.
- Homepage scroll height is long: about 10,743px desktop and 16,827px mobile in the baseline capture.
- Too many sections use card grids, creating visual repetition.
- Partner/lender presentation is verification-safe but still lacks enough premium spatial rhythm.
- Founder content is credible but could be more editorial and image-led.
- The calculator preview is useful but visually separated from the broader loan journey.
- Success-story and insight sections are content-safe placeholders, but visually similar to other cards.

## Crowded Sections

- Homepage service bento shows too many pathways at once.
- Audience tabs contain benefits and requirements at the same time; this is useful but visually dense.
- Document readiness plus process timeline plus calculator preview could be combined into one clearer planning journey.
- Footer is good but can feel link-heavy on mobile.

## Visually Weak Sections

- Hero lacks real human/business footage.
- Trust strip is text-only category pills.
- Service pages rely heavily on icon cards rather than image/story rhythm.
- Legal/resources pages remain largely text panels, which is acceptable but should receive better spacing.

## Repeated UI Patterns

- White cards with border/shadow appear across metrics, services, testimonials, blogs, pages and lender categories.
- Navy gradient sections appear often, but without enough distinct composition changes.
- Icon-led blocks repeat more often than image-led blocks.

## Mobile Issues

- No overflow was detected in the baseline screenshot pass.
- Mobile homepage is long and requires too much scrolling before reaching the strongest conversion points.
- Floating chat and sticky CTA are useful but must remain carefully spaced after the redesign.

## Sections To Combine

- Process timeline + EMI preview + document checklist can become a single "loan readiness journey".
- Success stories + trust copy can become a tighter trust/founder proof area.
- Blog showcase + FAQ can be compressed into a cleaner educational close.

## Sections To Rebuild

- Homepage hero: rebuild as cinematic video hero.
- Featured services: show fewer service pathways initially.
- Founder section: rebuild as editorial portrait/quote.
- Calculator preview: turn into a premium financial tool surface.
- Header: transparent over hero, solid on scroll.

## Page Recommendations

- Home: reduce visible density, introduce video, larger whitespace and layered visuals.
- About: keep concise, use image-led trust blocks.
- Founder: keep credentials factual, make the portrait and quote stronger.
- Services: preserve SEO routes, improve card hierarchy and category browsing.
- Service details: keep calculators and FAQs, make hero and sidebar more editorial.
- Calculators: retain logic, increase whitespace and strengthen result hierarchy.
- Apply: preserve validation, make onboarding feel calmer.
- Contact: make WhatsApp/call paths more direct.
- Partners: keep category-safe language, avoid unverified logos.
- FAQ/resources/legal: keep accessible and concise with better section spacing.
