# Final Design Direction

## Concept A: Premium Financial Consultancy

- Hero: editorial founder/trust-led consultancy message.
- Colors: deep navy, warm white, limited orange.
- Typography: large calm headlines, fewer labels.
- Visual style: founder portrait, meeting photography, paper/document detail.
- Sections: hero, founder trust, services, process, form.
- Motion: restrained fades and image reveals.
- CTA: consultation-first.
- Trust: CA-led credibility and relationship model.

## Concept B: Modern FinTech Platform

- Hero: financial dashboard and loan readiness interface.
- Colors: navy surfaces, white tool panels, orange active states.
- Typography: SaaS-like clarity.
- Visual style: calculator, eligibility score, document status, comparison UI.
- Sections: hero dashboard, tools, profile selector, product grid.
- Motion: animated numbers, tabs, sliders.
- CTA: calculate/check/apply.
- Trust: transparency, consent and structured process.

## Concept C: Cinematic DSA Brand

- Hero: business consultation video with strong overlay and premium campaign headline.
- Colors: cinematic navy overlay, soft white sections, small orange glow.
- Typography: large cinematic headline, short body copy.
- Visual style: video, layered cards, editorial service blocks.
- Sections: video hero, concise trust strip, featured services, planning journey, founder, final CTA.
- Motion: video, soft card float, scroll reveals.
- CTA: apply now and explore services.
- Trust: business guidance and long-term client relationships.

## Selected Direction

Use a hybrid direction:

- Parker Consultings' service clarity and consultation-forward navigation.
- Concept C for the cinematic first impression.
- Concept B for calculator and readiness tools.
- Concept A for founder and trust storytelling.

## Homepage Content Hierarchy

1. Cinematic Video Hero
2. Compact Trust Strip
3. Featured Services
4. Profile Selector
5. Loan Readiness Journey with calculator preview
6. Founder Trust Story
7. Document Preview
8. Selected insights and compact FAQ
9. Premium Final CTA
10. Clean Footer

## Content Condensing Rules

- Show fewer service cards on the homepage.
- Keep full service catalog on `/financial-services`.
- Move deeper SEO content to service pages.
- Keep homepage body copy short.
- Use tabs and interactive selectors for profile and documents.
- Keep placeholders clearly marked where approvals are missing.

## Visual System

- Primary surfaces: deep navy and dark ink.
- Supporting surfaces: warm white and light blue-grey.
- CTA/accent: orange and limited amber.
- Imagery: business consultation video, founder portrait, supplied Aura service imagery.
- Cards: fewer, larger and more editorial.
- Motion: controlled reveal, floating interface card, hover lift and reduced-motion support.
