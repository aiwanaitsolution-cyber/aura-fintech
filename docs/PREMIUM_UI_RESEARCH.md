# Premium UI Research

## Websites Inspected

Indian FinTech and lending: Paisabazaar, BankBazaar, Bajaj Finserv, Tata Capital, Lendingkart, Indifi, FlexiLoans, IIFL, Aditya Birla Capital, KreditBee, Navi, CRED, Groww, Razorpay and Slice.

Banking and finance: HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, IDFC FIRST Bank, AU Small Finance Bank, HSBC, DBS and Standard Chartered.

Premium SaaS and product inspiration: Stripe, Linear, Ramp, Brex, Mercury, Wise, Revolut, Webflow, Framer, Vercel, Notion and Deel.

Design galleries reviewed for directional patterns: Awwwards, Land-book, Lapa Ninja, SaaSFrame, Godly, One Page Love, Dribbble and Behance.

## Useful Patterns Found

- Stripe/Razorpay-style dashboard heroes communicate technology better than stock photos.
- Tata Capital and Bajaj Finserv use dense product pages with calculator, eligibility, documents, FAQs and clear Apply CTAs.
- Paisabazaar and BankBazaar emphasize comparison, trust, calculators and product breadth.
- CRED, Linear, Mercury and Ramp use premium dark surfaces, motion restraint, sharp typography and high-confidence spacing.
- Banking sites rely on trust signals, product tabs, service calculators and legal/disclosure clarity.
- Modern SaaS pages use bento grids, sticky navigation, dropdown mega menus, animated proof cards and strong final CTAs.

## Patterns Suitable For Aura Fintec

- Deep navy hero with a custom loan-readiness dashboard.
- Mega menu organized by services, calculators and education.
- Bento loan grid with varied card sizes.
- Audience tabs for salaried, self-employed, SMEs, manufacturers, startups and property owners.
- Dark EMI preview as a conversion bridge to the full calculator.
- Founder-led trust section for CA Ankita Garg with client-confirmation guardrails.
- Document checklist segmented by applicant type.
- Partner marquee with a visible unconfirmed-partner disclaimer.

## Patterns To Avoid

- Copying lender claims such as instant approval, lowest rates or guaranteed sanction.
- Overusing orange gradients, glass panels or generic finance icons.
- Making every service page a cloned card grid.
- Publishing bank/NBFC logos as confirmed partnerships before client approval.
- Heavy video backgrounds or animation that hurts mobile performance.

## Proposed Design Direction

Aura should feel like Stripe + Razorpay + Tata Capital + a premium CA-led consultancy: deep navy, orange accents, editorial Manrope headings, clean Inter body text, glass only as a supporting layer, custom finance dashboards, strong disclosure copy and compact conversion pathways.

## Proposed Homepage Flow

Premium sticky header, dashboard hero, partner marquee, metrics, bento service grid, audience tabs, animated process, EMI preview, founder spotlight, document checklist, testimonials, editorial resources, FAQ and final CTA.

## Proposed Animation Strategy

Use Framer Motion for hero entry, floating dashboard chips, bento hover, tabs, timeline reveal and testimonial hover. Use CSS for marquee, gradients and reduced-motion fallbacks. Avoid animation on every element.

## Proposed Mobile Experience

Mobile receives a full-screen menu, stacked hero dashboard, single-column bento cards, vertical timeline, sticky bottom Apply/Call actions, touch-friendly form steps, responsive charts and no horizontal overflow.

## Proposed Visual System

Deep navy gradients, warm off-white body background, blue-grey panels, orange/amber CTAs, soft shadows, 8-22px radius hierarchy, product-dashboard visuals, bento layout, segmented controls, premium footer and high-contrast focus states.
